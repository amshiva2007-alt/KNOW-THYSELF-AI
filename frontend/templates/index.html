The Philosophy

Why Fragmented Health Apps Fail You

Fitness trackers record steps. Smart beds record sleep. Phones monitor screen time. But nobody connects the dots.

You wake up tired with 8 hours in bed, but your fitness tracker doesn't know you spent 90 minutes scrolling social feeds in the dark. Isolated metrics hide the root causes of fatigue.

Know Thyself AI (Unified Intelligence)

We cross-analyze activity, screen friction, cardiovascular autonomic rhythm, and sleep architecture. You receive non-obvious, actionable lifestyle insights personalized to your unique physiology.
